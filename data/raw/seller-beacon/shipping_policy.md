# Shipping Policy

Seller: Beacon Outdoor Supply
Effective date: 1 February 2026
Version: 1

## Section 1 — Delivery times

Beacon Outdoor Supply ships within the selling country only.

Standard shipping takes 5 to 10 business days after a 2-business-day processing window.

## Section 2 — Fees

All orders pay a flat outbound shipping fee of $9.95. Beacon Outdoor Supply does not offer free shipping thresholds or express service.

## Section 3 — Lost packages

If a tracked package is confirmed lost by the carrier, Beacon Outdoor Supply will refund the merchandise value. Replacement shipments are not offered.

The customer pays return shipping in all cases, as stated in the Return Policy.
