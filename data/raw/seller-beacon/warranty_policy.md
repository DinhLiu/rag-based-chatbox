# Warranty Policy

Seller: Beacon Outdoor Supply
Effective date: 1 February 2026
Version: 1

## Section 1 — Coverage period

Beacon Outdoor Supply warrants products for 6 months from the delivery date.

## Section 2 — Covered events

This warranty covers manufacturing defects and accidental damage during normal outdoor use, including accidental water damage to covered products.

If a covered event is confirmed, Beacon Outdoor Supply will repair or replace the product.

## Section 3 — Claims

Customers must submit a claim with the order number and photographs before the 6-month period ends.
