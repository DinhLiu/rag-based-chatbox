# Return Policy

Seller: Beacon Outdoor Supply
Effective date: 1 February 2026
Version: 1

## Section 1 — Return period

Beacon Outdoor Supply accepts returns within 7 days of delivery.

The 7-day period starts on the carrier's delivery date.

## Section 2 — Condition and restocking

Returned products must be unused and in original packaging.

Change-of-mind returns are subject to a 15% restocking fee deducted from the refund.

## Section 3 — Return shipping

The customer pays all return shipping costs, including returns of items the customer believes are defective. Beacon Outdoor Supply does not prepay return labels.

## Section 4 — Refunds

Approved refunds, minus any restocking fee, are issued to the original payment method within 14 business days after the warehouse receives the return.
