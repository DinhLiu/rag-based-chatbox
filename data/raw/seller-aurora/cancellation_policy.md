# Order Cancellation Policy

Seller: Aurora Home Goods
Effective date: 15 January 2026
Version: 1

## Section 1 — Cancellation before dispatch

A customer may cancel an order at no charge before the order is dispatched.

To cancel, the customer must contact Customer Care with the order number while the order status is still awaiting dispatch.

## Section 2 — Cancellation after dispatch

After an order has been dispatched, it cannot be cancelled.

If the customer no longer wants the items after dispatch, the customer may use the Return Policy after the order is delivered, subject to that policy's period and condition rules.

## Section 3 — Seller-initiated cancellation

Aurora Home Goods may cancel an order before dispatch if an item is unavailable or if payment cannot be collected. In that case, any captured payment is refunded to the original payment method.
