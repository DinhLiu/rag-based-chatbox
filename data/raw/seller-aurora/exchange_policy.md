# Exchange Policy

Seller: Aurora Home Goods
Effective date: 15 January 2026
Version: 1

## Section 1 — Preference exchanges

A customer may exchange an unused product for a different size, color, or equivalent model within 14 days of delivery.

The original product must meet the same unused and original-packaging conditions described in the Return Policy.

The customer pays shipping both ways for preference exchanges.

## Section 2 — Defective-product exchanges

If a product is defective, the customer may request an exchange within 30 days of delivery.

Customer Care must confirm the defect from the customer's description and photographs before an exchange is approved.

For a confirmed defect, Aurora Home Goods pays shipping to return the defective product and shipping to send the replacement, as also stated in the Shipping Policy.

## Section 3 — Availability

If the requested exchange item is out of stock, Aurora Home Goods will issue a refund to the original payment method instead of completing the exchange.

## Section 4 — Cosmetic issues

Requests to exchange a product only because of minor cosmetic variation, such as a small difference in fabric shade, are reviewed case by case at Aurora Home Goods' discretion.

This policy does not define a standard outcome for those requests.
