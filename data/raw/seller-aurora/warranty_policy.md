# Warranty Policy

Seller: Aurora Home Goods
Effective date: 15 January 2026
Version: 1

## Section 1 — Coverage period

Aurora Home Goods warrants that products are free from manufacturing defects for 12 months from the delivery date.

## Section 2 — What the warranty covers

The warranty covers manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship that exist when the product is delivered or that appear during the 12-month period during normal household use.

If a covered defect is confirmed, Aurora Home Goods will repair the product or replace it with the same or a comparable item. The choice between repair and replacement belongs to Aurora Home Goods.

## Section 3 — What this warranty states it does not cover

This warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, including fading, pilling, or surface scratches from ordinary use.

This warranty does not cover damage caused by misuse, modification, or commercial use.

## Section 4 — How to make a claim

To make a warranty claim, the customer must contact Customer Care with the order number, a description of the defect, and photographs of the product.

Claims must be submitted before the 12-month coverage period ends.

## Section 5 — Limits of this document

This warranty only states coverage for manufacturing defects for 12 months.

It does not specify whether accidental damage, water damage, or other incidental harm is covered or excluded.
