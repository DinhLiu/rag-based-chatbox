# Package Protection Policy

Seller: Aurora Home Goods
Effective date: 15 January 2026
Version: 1

## Section 1 — Optional protection

Customers may add Package Protection for $2.99 per order at checkout.

Package Protection is optional and is not included automatically.

## Section 2 — What Package Protection covers

If Package Protection was purchased for the order, it covers theft of the package and confirmed loss of the package while it is in transit to the delivery address.

A covered claim results in a replacement shipment or a refund of the protected items.

## Section 3 — Claim window

A Package Protection claim must be submitted to Customer Care within 7 days of the original expected delivery date, with the order number and a description of the loss or theft.

## Section 4 — What this policy does not specify

This policy covers theft and confirmed in-transit loss only.

It does not specify coverage for damage after delivery, accidental damage during product use, or water damage.
