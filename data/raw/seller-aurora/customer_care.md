# Customer Care Procedures

Seller: Aurora Home Goods
Effective date: 15 January 2026
Version: 1

## Section 1 — Contact

Customers may email support@aurorahomegoods.example with their order number.

Aurora Home Goods does not offer telephone support.

## Section 2 — Hours and response time

Customer Care reads messages Monday through Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 UTC, excluding store holidays.

Email replies are sent within 2 business days.

## Section 3 — What Customer Care can do

Customer Care can review return, warranty, shipping, exchange, payment, cancellation, and package-protection requests that are described in the store's published policies.

When a request is not clearly covered by those policies, Customer Care will say so and will not invent a store rule.

## Section 4 — Information required

Customers should include the order number, product name, and relevant photographs when asking about defects, missing items, or returns.
