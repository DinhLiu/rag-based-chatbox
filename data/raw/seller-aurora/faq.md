# Frequently Asked Questions

Seller: Aurora Home Goods
Effective date: 15 January 2026
Version: 1

## Section 1 — Can I send a product back if I change my mind?

Yes, if the product is unused, still in its original packaging, and the request is made within 14 days of delivery. See the Return Policy for the full condition and shipping rules.

## Section 2 — How long does a refund take?

After the warehouse inspects an approved return, refunds to cards take 5 to 10 business days. PayPal refunds take 3 to 5 business days. See the Payment Policy and Return Policy.

## Section 3 — How long is the warranty?

Manufacturing defects are covered for 12 months from delivery. See the Warranty Policy for claim steps and the limits of that coverage.

## Section 4 — Can I cancel after the order has shipped?

No. After dispatch, the order cannot be cancelled. You may use the Return Policy after delivery if the product qualifies. See the Order Cancellation Policy.

## Section 5 — Who pays shipping if I exchange a defective product?

If Customer Care confirms a defect, Aurora Home Goods pays the return and replacement shipping. See the Exchange Policy and Shipping Policy.

This FAQ restates published store rules. It does not add coverage, fees, or exceptions beyond those policies.
