# Payment Policy

Seller: Aurora Home Goods
Effective date: 15 January 2026
Version: 1

## Section 1 — Accepted methods

Aurora Home Goods accepts Visa, Mastercard, and PayPal.

Cash on delivery is not offered.

## Section 2 — When payment is collected

The customer's selected payment method is charged when the order is dispatched.

## Section 3 — Refunds

Refunds are sent only to the original payment method used for the order.

Card refunds appear within 5 to 10 business days after Aurora Home Goods issues the refund.

PayPal refunds appear within 3 to 5 business days after Aurora Home Goods issues the refund.

## Section 4 — Failed payments

If payment cannot be collected at dispatch, the order is not shipped and is cancelled.

This policy does not describe installment plans, store credit, or gift-card purchase rules.
