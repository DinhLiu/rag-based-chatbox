# Shipping Policy

Seller: Aurora Home Goods
Effective date: 15 January 2026
Version: 1

## Section 1 — Service area

Aurora Home Goods ships to addresses within the same country as the selling store.

This policy does not describe international shipping, duties, or cross-border delivery times.

## Section 2 — Processing and delivery times

Orders are processed within 1 to 2 business days after payment is confirmed.

Standard shipping then takes 3 to 7 business days after processing.

Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days after processing.

## Section 3 — Outbound shipping fees

Standard outbound shipping costs $6.95.

Outbound shipping is free on orders with a merchandise total of $50 or more before tax.

Express shipping adds a $12 fee in addition to any standard shipping charge that would otherwise apply. The $50 free-shipping threshold does not waive the express fee.

## Section 4 — Lost packages

All orders include carrier tracking.

If tracking shows a package as lost, Aurora Home Goods will open a carrier investigation. If the carrier confirms the loss, Aurora Home Goods will send a replacement or issue a refund for the missing items.

This policy does not describe compensation for late delivery when the package is not lost.

## Section 5 — Defective-item return shipping

When Customer Care confirms that a delivered product has a manufacturing defect, Aurora Home Goods pays the shipping cost to return that product and the outbound shipping cost of any replacement.

Preference-based returns and exchanges follow the Return Policy and Exchange Policy for shipping responsibility.
