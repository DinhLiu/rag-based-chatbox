# Return Policy

Seller: Aurora Home Goods
Effective date: 15 January 2026
Version: 1

## Section 1 — Purpose

This policy describes when a customer may return a product purchased from Aurora Home Goods and how refunds are issued.

## Section 2 — Return period

Products may be returned within 14 days of delivery.

The 14-day period starts on the calendar date shown on the carrier's delivery confirmation.

## Section 3 — Condition of returned goods

To be eligible, returned products must be unused, in their original packaging, and include all original tags, manuals, and accessories that were supplied with the order.

Products that show signs of use, missing parts, or damaged packaging are not eligible under this return policy.

## Section 4 — Return shipping responsibility

The customer pays the return shipping cost for change-of-mind returns and for returns of products that arrived as ordered.

Aurora Home Goods pays return shipping when the delivered product is the wrong item or has a manufacturing defect that is reported on arrival.

## Section 5 — Refund method and timing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method only.

After the warehouse receives and inspects the return, refund processing takes 5 to 10 business days.

## Section 6 — Scope

This policy covers unused products returned within the period and condition rules above.

It does not describe returns of discounted, promotional, or final-sale items. It does not describe returns requested after the 14-day period.
